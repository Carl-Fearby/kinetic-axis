# Review request templates

Replace `{{first_name}}`, `{{project_name}}` and `{{google_review_link}}` before sending. Keep the tone direct and low-pressure. Do not offer an incentive, and do not ask only clients who are likely to be positive.

## 1. Initial request (send within 48 hours of a successful handover)

**Subject:** A quick favour, {{first_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks again for the work on {{project_name}}. It has been good to see it in use.

If you have a couple of minutes, a short Google review would genuinely help other local businesses find us. There is no need to write an essay — a sentence about what changed or what the process was like is enough.

{{google_review_link}}

No pressure at all if you would rather not. And if anything is still not right, tell me first and we will sort it.

Thanks,
Carl
Kinetic Axis
07971 639927

## 2. One reminder (5–7 days later, only if there was no reply)

**Subject:** Re: A quick favour, {{first_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Just bringing this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried. If you are happy to leave a short review, the link is here:

{{google_review_link}}

If it is not a good time, no problem — I will leave it there.

Thanks,
Carl

## 3. Short SMS or WhatsApp version

Hi {{first_name}}, Carl from Kinetic Axis. Thanks again for {{project_name}}. If you have a minute, a short Google review would really help other local businesses find us: {{google_review_link}}. No pressure either way — and tell me first if anything needs fixing. Thanks.

## 4. If the client raises a problem instead of reviewing

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for telling me — I would much rather know than have you feel you had to leave a review about it. Let's get it sorted first. Could we have a quick call {{suggested_time}}? If it is easier, reply with what is not working and I will come back with a fix or a plan.

Carl

Do not send the review link again until the issue is resolved and the client is genuinely satisfied.

## 5. Response to a positive review

Thanks {{first_name}} — really glad {{specific_detail}} worked well. It was a good project to work on, and I appreciate you taking the time to say so. Carl

## 6. Response to a mixed review

Thanks for the feedback, {{first_name}}. I'm glad {{positive_detail}} landed well, and I'm sorry {{specific_issue}} did not meet the mark. I'd like to understand what happened and put it right — please email hello@kineticaxisdigital.co.uk and I'll pick it up personally. Carl

## 7. Response to a critical review

I'm sorry this fell short. I'd like to understand what happened and see what we can do to put it right. Please email hello@kineticaxisdigital.co.uk with the project details and I'll respond personally. Carl

Do not disclose confidential project information, argue about the facts in public or suggest the reviewer is not a real client.

## 8. Testimonial permission (if you want to quote the client on the site)

**Subject:** Permission to quote your feedback

Hi {{first_name}},

Thank you for the feedback — it was genuinely useful. Would you be happy for us to quote part of it on the Kinetic Axis website, attributed to you and {{company}}? We would only use the words you approve, and you can withdraw permission at any time.

If yes, reply with the exact wording you are comfortable with (or ask us to send a proposed edit).

Thanks,
Carl

## Rules

- One initial request and one reminder only.
- No incentives, prizes, discounts or reciprocal reviews.
- No review gating (do not ask only happy clients).
- Never write or edit a review on a client's behalf.
- Record the date, client and outcome so requests are not repeated.
- Stop immediately if the client asks you to.
